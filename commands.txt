
#############
### RULES ###
#############
Prefix: 
- '$' 
- For example '$kek'

HEALTH:
- Start with 100 HP. When HP reaches 0, you die for 3 hours (you can only view your stats, portfolio, hp and xp).
- Dying to $steal or $duel: lose ALL keks to the attacker.
- When you respawn your HP back to 25.

ACTIVITY:
- You cannot be targeted by $steal/$duel if inactive 15+ minutes.
- Activity = any message sent to chat. Timer resets each message.

CURRENCY and LEVELING:
- Keks (🍪): spent on $eat (5 keks per HP), $buy stocks, $duel wagering.
- Keks gained from: $bonus (daily), $coinflip (win), $steal (success, 35% rate), $duel (win).
- XP gained only from $steal (+25) and $duel win (+50). Not from $bonus.
- Leveling: 1000 XP per level, max level 30.
- Keks Bonus: +1 kek per level. Level 1 = +1 kek, Level 10 = +10 keks, etc.
  Applies to $bonus gains and $steal loot only (not $duel wagers or losses).
- Example: Level 3 stealing 100 keks = 103 keks gained.

COMBAT BASICS
- Damage roll: 1 to min(10, target_hp) HP.
- Level advantage: ~+0.3% win chance per level difference (±15% cap), +2% damage bonus per level (+30% cap).

STEAL
- Command: $steal <target> (no amount).
- Needs: 10+ keks, target active, no cooldown active.
- Success (35%): Deal damage, gain XP, take their keks (all if they die).
- Failure (65%): Take damage, lose keks, no XP.
- Cooldown: 30 min (win or lose).

DUEL
- Command: $duel <target> <amount> (creates challenge).
- Opponent must type $accept within 60 sec (or $accept <your_name> if multiple pending).
- Needs: Both alive, both have wager amount, target active.
- Outcome: Coin flip (skewed by level), random damage to loser.
- If loser HP ≤ 0: dies, loses all keks. Otherwise: loses wager amount.
- Both gain +50 XP (winner only).
- Win/loss tracked in stats.

BONUS DAILY
- Command: $kek (or $bonus). Cooldown: 30 min.
- Outcome: 5% nothing, ~75% gain 1-30 keks (plus level bonus), ~20% lose 1-30 keks (no bonus).

OTHER MECHANICS
- $send: Transfer keks to another player (no cooldown, no bonus).
- $coinflip: 50/50 bet (no bonus).
- $eat: Spend 5 keks per HP to heal self or another player (you pay).
- $stocks/$buy/$sell: No bonus on stock transactions.
- $convert: Turn XP into keks (10 xp → 1 kek, one-way). Lowers your level.

#######################
### GETTING STARTED ###
#######################

$kek (alias: $bonus)
  Claim your daily reward. Registers you the first time you use it.
  Cooldown: 30 min. Most of the time you gain keks, sometimes you get 0,
  and sometimes you lose keks (occasionally to a random other player).

######################
### OTHER COMMANDS ###
######################

$send <user> <amount|all> (aliases: $sendkeks, $give, $givekeks)
  Send keks to another player. Can't send to yourself or a dead player.

$coinflip <heads|tails> <amount|all>
  Bet keks on a 50/50 coin flip.

$stocks (aliases: $price, $prices)
  List all stocks and their current price.

$portfolio
  Show the stocks you own and their total value.

$buy <stock> <amount|all>
  Buy shares of a stock (max 100 shares per stock).

$sell <stock> <amount|all>
  Sell shares of a stock.

$holders <stock>
  List who owns a stock and how many shares

$steal <user>
  Try to steal keks from another player. Costs nothing to attempt, but:
  - You need at least 10 keks to attempt a steal.
  - The target must have been active in the last 15 minutes.
  - 30 min cooldown between attempts, win or lose.
  - On success (35% rate) you deal damage, gain XP, and take their keks
    (all of it if the damage kills them). On failure you take damage and
    lose keks instead, with no XP gained

$duel <user> <amount>
  Challenge another player to a duel for a kek wager. The target must have
  been active in the last 15 minutes. This only creates a challenge - the
  target has to type $accept to actually fight. The challenge expires
  after 60 seconds if not accepted.

$accept <challenger (optional)>
  Accept a pending duel challenge sent to you. If you have more than one
  pending challenge, specify who you're accepting, e.g. $accept alice.
  The fight is a coin flip skewed slightly by level (higher level = small
  edge in win chance and damage) and deals random damage to the loser.
  If the loser's HP hits 0 they die and drop all their keks to the winner,
  otherwise the winner just takes the wager. Both fighters must be alive
  and able to cover the wager at accept time.

$eat <amount|full> or $eat <user> <amount|full> (alias: $heal)
  Heal HP by spending keks (5 keks per HP). Without a target you heal
  yourself; with a target you pay to heal someone else instead.

$hp <user (optional)>
  Show HP bar for yourself or another player.

$xp <user (optional)> (alias: $level)
  Show level, XP bar/progress, and current kek bonus per gain.

$convert <xp amount|all>
  Convert XP into keks, one-way only (10 xp -> 1 kek). Lowering your XP
  can lower your level and kek bonus, so spend it deliberately.

$stats <user (optional)> (alias: $keks)
  Full stat summary: HP, respawn timer, level/XP, duel wins/losses,
  balance, bonus/steal cooldowns, coinflip stats, and portfolio.

$leaderboard (aliases: $top, $kekkers)
  Top 10 richest players.

$bottom
  Bottom 10 poorest players.

$graveyard
  List all dead players and how long until each one respawns.

$commands
  Link to the full commands list.
